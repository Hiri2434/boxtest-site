Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Box Test is an agility cardio test app made by HiriDev. This policy covers what the app stores, where it stores it, and your choices. The short version: without an account, nothing leaves your phone. With an account, we store only what's needed to back up and sync your runs, and you can delete it any time from inside the app.

Without an account

Everything stays on your phone: your runs, stars and badges, and your profile (username, optional full name, birth year and month, region, sports, and the optional weight and sex used for calorie estimates). Saved players you add for group runs also stay only on your phone. We don't receive any of it.

With an account (optional)

You can create an account (with Google, or with your email address and a sign-in code) to back up your runs, use them on another phone, and connect with other athletes. When you do, we store:

  • Your sign-in: your email address and, if you use Google, a Google account identifier, used only to sign you in.
  • Your profile: username, a short bio and a profile photo (if you add them), full name (if you entered one), birth year and month, country, province/state and city (if chosen), sports, and your leaderboard setting.
  • Your runs and badges: each run's result, settings, date and stars, and the badges you've earned.
  • Runs with others: when someone adds you to a Survivor run or Squads relay by your username, your result from that run is saved to your account, along with the usernames and levels of the others on it. You can remove any run you were added to, or tell the person it's wrong.
  • Follows and privacy choices: who you follow, who follows you, follow requests, the people you block, and your "Who can add me to runs" setting.
  • Your groups: the groups you create, join or ask to join, and your role in each. If you report a username, a profile photo or bio, or a group, we keep the report so we can review it.

We do not upload your weight or sex, any health data (see Health Connect below), your device settings, or any saved players you add for group runs.

Accounts are only for people at or above the minimum age for their country (13 in most places, higher in some, e.g. 16 in Germany). The app checks this before sign-in, and our servers refuse accounts below it.

What others can see

Your full name, birth date, email and place are never shown on your profile. Other people can see only:

  • Your username is visible to signed-in users wherever it appears (search, leaderboards, groups, runs you were added to). Usernames are unique.
  • Your profile page: for adults, your photo, bio, best level, personal bests and recent runs are visible to signed-in users, and anyone can follow you. For anyone under 18, these are visible only to followers you have approved; everyone else sees just your username.
  • Player search: adults can be found by username unless they turn off "Who can add me to runs". People under 18 are hidden from search by default; if they turn it on, they can be found only by typing their exact username.
  • Leaderboards: if your leaderboard setting is on, your username, age group, best level and region. The setting is on by default for adults and off for anyone under 18 until they turn it on. For anyone under 18, a city is never shown.
  • Groups: members of a group you belong to see your username, age group and sports. The group's coaches and admins can also see your synced runs, including runs from before you joined, until you leave the group.

Group names and descriptions are visible in group search unless the group is unlisted.

Health Connect (Android)

If you choose to connect Box Test to Health Connect, Box Test saves each run you complete as a workout (its start and end, laps, distance and estimated calories) so it shows up in apps like Samsung Health and Google Health, and reads heart-rate readings recorded by your watch or other apps for the time of your run, so it can show them with the run and improve the calorie estimate. Box Test reads nothing else from Health Connect, does not read in the background, and reads only heart rate from the 30 days around a run. This health data, and the optional weight and sex you can add to your profile for calorie estimates, stay on your phone: they are never sent to the Box Test server or shared with anyone. You can turn saving off in Settings at any time and remove access in Health Connect, and deleting a run in Box Test deletes its workout from Health Connect.

Where it's stored

Account data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, in its Canada (Central) region. Profile photos are stored privately in the same place. Sign-in with Google is handled by Google under Google's own privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no selling or sharing of your data.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No messaging between users.
  • No sharing of your profile photo or bio outside Box Test; profile photos are stored privately and shown only through the app, under the rules above.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything synced to it from our servers. You can choose to keep your runs on your phone or remove them too. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the phone.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
