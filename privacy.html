Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Box Test is an agility cardio test app. This policy covers what the app stores, where it stores it, and your choices. The short version: without an account, nothing leaves your phone. With an account, we store only what's needed to back up and sync your runs, and you can delete it any time from inside the app.

Without an account

Everything stays on your phone: your runs, stars and badges, and your profile (nickname, optional full name, birth year and month, region, sports, and the optional weight and sex used for calorie estimates). Saved players you add for group runs also stay only on your phone. We don't receive any of it.

With an account (optional)

You can create an account with Google to back up your runs and use them on another phone. When you do, we store:

  • Your sign-in: your email address and a Google account identifier, used only to sign you in.
  • Your profile: nickname, full name (if you entered one), birth year and month, country, province/state and city (if chosen), sports, and your leaderboard setting.
  • Your runs and badges: each run's result, settings, date and stars, and the badges you've earned.
  • Your groups: the groups you create, join or ask to join, and your role in each. If you report a nickname or a group, we keep the report so we can review it.

We do not upload your weight or sex, any health data (see Health Connect below), your device settings, or any saved players you add for group runs.

Accounts are only for people at or above the minimum age for their country (13 in most places, higher in some, e.g. 16 in Germany). The app checks this before sign-in, and our servers refuse accounts below it.

What others can see

Your full name and birth date are never shown to anyone. Other people can see only:

  • Leaderboards: if your leaderboard setting is on, your nickname, age group, best level and region. The setting is on by default for adults and off for anyone under 18 until they turn it on. For anyone under 18, a city is never shown.
  • Groups: members of a group you belong to see your nickname, age group and sports. The group's coaches and admins can also see your synced runs, including runs from before you joined, until you leave the group.

Group names and descriptions are visible in group search unless the group is unlisted.

Health Connect (Android)

If you choose to connect Box Test to Health Connect, Box Test saves each run you complete as a workout (its start and end, laps, distance and estimated calories) so it shows up in apps like Samsung Health and Google Health, and reads heart-rate readings recorded by your watch or other apps for the time of your run, so it can show them with the run and improve the calorie estimate. Box Test reads nothing else from Health Connect, does not read in the background, and reads only heart rate from the 30 days around a run. This health data, and the optional weight and sex you can add to your profile for calorie estimates, stay on your phone: they are never sent to the Box Test server or shared with anyone. You can turn saving off in Settings at any time and remove access in Health Connect, and deleting a run in Box Test deletes its workout from Health Connect.

Where it's stored

Account data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, in its Canada (Central) region. Sign-in with Google is handled by Google under Google's own privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no selling or sharing of your data.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No messaging between users.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything synced to it from our servers. You can choose to keep your runs on your phone or remove them too. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the phone.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
