Box Test

Agility cardio, measured.

Hear a cone, sprint to it

Set four cones in a 5 × 5 m box. The app calls a cone number; you sprint to it and back before the next call. Every level gets faster.

Stars, badges and personal bests

Each run is scored against your own recent form, with badges for milestones and age groups from U9 to Adult.

Run with others

Squads: a relay in one box, one level each, every runner at their own age group's pace. Survivor: a whole team head-to-head on identical boxes.

Groups and leaderboards

Join your school, team or club, and see how you rank in your group, city, province, country and the world, within your age group.

Works with your health apps

On Android, runs save to Samsung Health, Google Health and more through Health Connect, with calories and heart rate from your watch.

Optional account

Back up your runs and use them on another phone. Without an account, nothing leaves your phone.
